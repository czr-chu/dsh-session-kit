# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be harassment-free for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive in issues, pull requests and discussions.
- Assume good intent; critique code and ideas, not people.
- Do not publish other people's session data, credentials, private paths or
  screenshots. Redact logs and archives before sharing them.
- Follow the security policy for vulnerabilities instead of opening a public
  issue.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, publishing private data, or
sustained disruption of project spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently
ban participants for behavior that violates this policy. Reports can be sent
privately to the maintainer listed in the repository profile. Reports will be
handled confidentially.

This policy is adapted from the spirit of the Contributor Covenant.
